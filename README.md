## 👨‍💻 About Me

🎓 **5th-year IT engineering student at CESI Engineering School (Nancy, France)**, focused on **data engineering and applied machine learning**.

📊 I work across the whole chain: collecting and cleaning data, building reliable pipelines, training interpretable models, and delivering results the business can use through dashboards and APIs.

🔬 **AI research intern** at UTCN (Cluj-Napoca, Romania): deep reinforcement learning (PPO) for cloud resource allocation and a hybrid BDI + RL architecture for autonomous driving.
